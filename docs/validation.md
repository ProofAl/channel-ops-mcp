# What the first demand checks established

Research date: November 24, 2026. The unit is an independent team operating a YouTube channel with an off-platform offer. The free self-hosted tool and the assisted service have separate economics.

| Claim tested | Evidence and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Connecting YouTube data to both ChatGPT and Claude is distinctive | Rejected. vidIQ offers both on its free plan and supports metadata editing. The pilot therefore leads with campaign contribution and a report based on the team's own sales. [vidIQ MCP](https://vidiq.com/mcp/) |
| Some teams already track a video through to an off-platform sale | Supported as a workflow in a documented case. A former CareerFoundry video lead describes tracking visits, course leads and enrollments. One practitioner case cannot establish market size or willingness to pay. [Practitioner account](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R7NPyfI5lDA) |
| Teams will pay $29 to get a usable report for one channel | Open. Count only a settled first payment. Record delivery time, cash costs and the team's return after seven days. The first cohort and review date are public. [Pilot experiment](https://github.com/ProofAl/channel-ops-mcp/issues/1) |

Measure acquisition using an identified placement and its tagged link. Log qualified exposure when a distinct external team actually considers the offer. A repository view, star, software test, owner walkthrough or automated request does not qualify. Deduplicate repeat visits within the cohort. Save customer-level evidence privately and publish only aggregate results.

The public demo performs its calculation without a marketing tracker. Repository traffic and voluntary pilot applications cover different denominators. Report unavailable visitor-to-install and install-to-payment conversions as unknown. A pilot request records interest. Confirm use and payment separately with first-party evidence.

If the first review arrives before ten qualified teams have seen the offer, the demand result is inconclusive. If ten qualify but fewer than three can build a report, examine onboarding with those teams. If the reports succeed and no one pays, test the buyer, the offer and assistance time before adding platform features.


## Decision discovery

In an interview with Michael Kitces, Troy Sharpe describes callers who watch the firm's YouTube videos, find the business through search and call. Employees ask how callers heard about them and record the channel in their system. An exact episode can remain unknown. Sam Oh describes a different editorial decision at Ahrefs: choosing topics for both business value and audience potential. These are operator accounts about their businesses. [Oak Harvest interview](https://www.kitces.com/blog/troy-sharpe-marketing-youtube-videos-growth-aum-roi/) · [Ahrefs interview](https://www.napierb2b.com/2022/12/a-napier-podcast-interview-with-sam-oh-ahrefs/)

The [creator survey](https://channel-ops-lab.knowingfoxglove4.chatgpt.site/#survey) asks which decision was hardest after the team's latest release. Responses are voluntary. A receipt suppresses repeated submissions in the same browser under normal use. It cannot identify unique people or teams. Review the distribution by decision, role and current method. Keep the vote count separate from qualified teams, own-data reports, seven-day returns and settled payments in the paid pilot.

If one decision is chosen by at least five of the first ten valid answers, prioritize that decision for interviews. Otherwise review the split and ask for concrete recent examples from pilot volunteers. Interview evidence should describe what the customer actually did, how much work it took and what changed after seeing a report. A vote is a prioritization signal. It does not validate pricing.
